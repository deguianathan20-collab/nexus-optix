Frequently Asked Questions

Multi-Marketplace FAQ

Which marketplaces should my brand sell on beyond Amazon?
It depends on your product category, target demographic, and growth goals. Walmart is essential for scale, Target for premium positioning, TikTok Shop for younger audiences, and platforms like eBay, Instacart, and Kroger for specific verticals. Nexus Brand Group provides a marketplace selection analysis to identify your best opportunities.
How do you manage inventory across multiple marketplaces?
We use cross-platform inventory management tools and custom integrations to sync stock levels, prevent overselling, and optimize fulfillment routing across all your active channels. This includes coordinating FBA, WFS, and other fulfillment programs.
What is the benefit of a multi-marketplace strategy?
Diversifying across marketplaces reduces dependence on any single platform, increases total addressable market, and builds brand resilience. Brands selling on 3+ marketplaces typically see 30–50% higher total revenue compared to single-platform sellers.
Do you manage Instacart and grocery marketplace advertising?
Yes. We manage Instacart Ads, Kroger Precision Marketing, and other grocery and retail media platforms. These channels are especially valuable for CPG, food and beverage, health and wellness, and household product brands.
Can you consolidate reporting across all our marketplace channels?
Absolutely. Our Optix analytics platform provides unified cross-marketplace reporting, giving you a single dashboard view of performance across Amazon, Walmart, Target, TikTok Shop, and other channels. This eliminates the need to log into multiple platforms for performance insights.
