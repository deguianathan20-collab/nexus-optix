Frequently Asked Questions

Marketplace Services FAQ

What marketplaces does Nexus Brand Group manage?
Nexus Brand Group provides full-service management for Amazon (Seller Central and Vendor Central), Walmart Marketplace, Target Plus, TikTok Shop, eBay, Instacart, Kroger, and emerging retail platforms. We handle advertising, catalog management, fulfillment strategy, and analytics across all channels.
What makes Nexus different from other marketplace agencies?
Three things set us apart: our team's deep ex-Amazon expertise (including our founder's 11+ years at Amazon), our proprietary Optix analytics platform that provides real-time performance visibility, and our true partnership model where your success directly drives ours.
How do I know if my brand needs a marketplace agency?
If you're spending more than 10 hours per week on marketplace operations, struggling with advertising efficiency, seeing stagnant growth, or want to expand to new platforms, an agency can accelerate your results. Brands typically see ROI within the first 90 days of working with Nexus.
What does onboarding with Nexus Brand Group look like?
Onboarding includes a comprehensive brand audit, competitive analysis, advertising account review, catalog assessment, and strategy development. We set clear KPIs and growth targets in the first 30 days, with full management takeover within 2–3 weeks.
Do you offer a free marketplace consultation?
Yes. We offer a no-obligation consultation where we review your current marketplace presence, identify growth opportunities, and outline a recommended strategy. Schedule your free consultation on our contact page.
