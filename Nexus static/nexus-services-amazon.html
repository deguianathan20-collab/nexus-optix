Frequently Asked Questions

Amazon Agency FAQ

What does a full-service Amazon agency do?
A full-service Amazon agency manages every aspect of your brand's presence on Amazon — including account management, advertising (PPC and DSP), catalog optimization, listing SEO, inventory planning, brand protection, and performance analytics. Nexus Brand Group handles all of this so your team can focus on product development and supply chain.
How much does Amazon agency management cost?
Amazon agency fees vary based on your catalog size, advertising budget, and service scope. Most agencies charge a monthly management fee plus a percentage of ad spend. Contact Nexus Brand Group for a custom proposal tailored to your brand's needs and growth goals.
What's the difference between Seller Central and Vendor Central management?
Seller Central (3P) means you sell directly to consumers through Amazon, controlling pricing and inventory. Vendor Central (1P) means you sell wholesale to Amazon, who then resells to consumers. Nexus Brand Group manages both models and can advise on which is best for your brand.
How does Nexus Brand Group measure Amazon advertising performance?
We track ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sale), TACoS (Total Advertising Cost of Sale), ROAS, impression share, conversion rates, and organic rank improvements. Our proprietary Optix dashboard gives you real-time visibility into all performance metrics.
Can you help with Amazon brand protection and MAP enforcement?
Yes. We monitor your listings for unauthorized sellers, counterfeit products, listing hijackers, and MAP violations. Our brand protection services include takedown requests, cease and desist coordination, and Amazon Brand Registry optimization.
How long does it take to see results from Amazon agency management?
Most brands see measurable improvements in advertising efficiency within 30–60 days. Organic ranking improvements and catalog optimization results typically emerge within 60–90 days. Long-term brand growth compounds over 6–12 months.
