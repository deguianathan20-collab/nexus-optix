About Nexus Brand Group

The Marketplace Agency Built by Amazon Insiders

Nexus Brand Group is a full-service Amazon and marketplace management agency headquartered in the United States. Founded in 2016 by Chris Purcell, a former Amazon executive with over 11 years of experience at the company, Nexus provides end-to-end brand management across Amazon, Walmart, Target, TikTok Shop, and other leading ecommerce platforms.

Since its founding, Nexus Brand Group has managed over $3 billion in marketplace revenue for its clients, directed more than $100 million in advertising spend, and optimized over 200,000 product listings. The agency serves brands of all sizes across the United States, from emerging DTC brands to established consumer packaged goods companies.

Nexus Brand Group offers marketplace strategy, advertising management (including Amazon PPC, Amazon DSP, Walmart Connect, and Target Roundel), catalog and listing optimization, inventory and fulfillment planning, brand protection, and real-time analytics through its proprietary Optix dashboard platform.

The agency operates on a true partnership model, aligning its incentives directly with client growth outcomes rather than charging flat management fees alone. This performance-driven approach has made Nexus one of the fastest-growing marketplace agencies in the United States.
