Frequently Asked Questions

TikTok FAQ

What is TikTok and should my brand be on it?
TikTok commerce is TikTok's built-in ecommerce platform that lets brands sell products directly through the app via shoppable videos, live streams, and a product showcase tab. If your brand targets consumers under 45 and your products are visually compelling, TikTok is a high-growth channel worth investing in.
How does TikTok advertising work?
TikTok commerce ads include Video Shopping Ads (promote shoppable videos in the feed), LIVE Shopping Ads (drive viewers to live selling events), and Product Shopping Ads (display in commerce placements). Nexus manages all ad formats to drive discovery and conversion.
What is the TikTok affiliate program?
TikTok's affiliate program connects brands with content creators who promote products in exchange for commission on sales. Nexus Brand Group identifies and recruits high-performing creators, manages affiliate partnerships, and optimizes commission structures to maximize ROI.
Do you manage TikTok live selling?
Yes. We develop live selling strategies, coordinate live event scheduling, manage real-time engagement tactics, and optimize post-live performance. Live selling on TikTok has shown conversion rates 3–5x higher than standard video shopping.
How quickly can a brand launch on TikTok?
TikTok commerce account setup takes 1–2 weeks. Catalog integration, content strategy development, and initial affiliate outreach add 2–3 weeks. Most brands are fully live with advertising within 4–6 weeks of engagement with Nexus Brand Group.
